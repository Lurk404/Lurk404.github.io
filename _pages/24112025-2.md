Consider the top 5 most impactful accessibility considerations

## Headings

Create a hierarchical heading structure, for example:

Heading 1 (title)

Heading 2 (section)

Heading 3 (subsection)

Heading 2 (section)

## Colour and Fonts

Dark font on light background or light on dark

Sans-serif, with serif sparingly

![Image of the letter a in upper and lower case in sans-serif and serif font styles. The serifs—small ticks at the end of character strokes—are circled in red.](/assets/images/wp/2025/09/serifs.png)

Do not use colour alone to differentiate or indicate

## Alternative Text

If an image contains information, assume viewers can't see it

![Screenshot of digital branching scenario overview showing the various branches and end scenarios](/assets/images/wp/2024/10/Picture713.png)
