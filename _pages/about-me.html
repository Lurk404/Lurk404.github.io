My name is Luke McKnight. I am a digital accessibility specialist, an IAAP certified Accessible Document Specialist, and a Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies. I am a Master of Design in Inclusive Design.

I work with students to find technology solutions that help them learn and demonstrate what they have learned. I also assist post-secondary staff with creating accessible content, specifically in common learning technologies such as Brightspace and PebblePad. Nearly everything I write I give away for free.
