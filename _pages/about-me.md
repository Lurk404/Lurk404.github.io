My name is Luke McKnight. I am a digital accessibility specialist, an IAAP certified [Accessible Document Specialist](https://www.accessibilityassociation.org/s/accessible-document-specialist), and a [Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies](https://www.accessibilityassociation.org/s/certified-professional). I hold a Master of Design in [Inclusive Design](https://gradadmissions.ocadu.ca/program/INCD) from OCAD University.
