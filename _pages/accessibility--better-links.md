## Persona

![](/assets/images/wp/2024/03/jacob.jpg)

My name is Jacob and I’m a fourth-year business administration student who is blind and a confident technology user.

I use a screen-reading program (JAWS) to access electronic text materials, as well as an audio-recorder to “take notes” in class.

I am 28 years old and can’t wait to get my last few classes out of the way so I can start my career!

I share an apartment with my girlfriend.

Image and persona courtesy [Student Personas](https://udl4camosun.opened.ca/student-persona-resources/meet-the-students/). Used under [CC-BY-4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

---

Jacob uses headings and links to browse and skim webpages. Writing informative link text is essential for Jacob but helpful for all users.

## Hyperlinks

Accessible link text is essential for screen reader users to navigate content. Better written link text also helps all users understand the purpose and destination of links.

| Bad Link Writing | Better Link Writing |
| --- | --- |
| Read [this article](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/article/2024/may/13/campus-gaza-protests-student-protesters-role-history) about how US student protests have taken the morally correct stance throughout history. | The Guardian reports that throughout history [American student protests have chosen the morally just side](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/article/2024/may/13/campus-gaza-protests-student-protesters-role-history). |
| Not only are the students morally just, remember they are protesting in part because Israel has destroyed every university in Gaza, [read more here](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/1/24/how-israel-has-destroyed-gazas-schools-and-universities#why-every-university-in-gaza-destroyed). | Attempts to discredit student protests are largely an intentional effort to distract from [Israel's intentional destruction of every university in Gaza](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/1/24/how-israel-has-destroyed-gazas-schools-and-universities#why-every-university-in-gaza-destroyed). |
| [Click here](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2024/04/un-experts-deeply-concerned-over-scholasticide-gaza) to read the UN's concern about scholasticide. | United Nations experts are deeply concerned with [Israel's deliberate scholasticide](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2024/04/un-experts-deeply-concerned-over-scholasticide-gaza). |
| For any conscious person, Israel's slaughter of Palestinians is at minimum <https://www.un.org/en/genocideprevention/ethnic-cleansing.shtml>. | Israel's slaughter of Palestinians meets the [United Nations definition of ethnic cleansing](https://www.un.org/en/genocideprevention/ethnic-cleansing.shtml). |
| To learn more about the ethnic cleansing of Palestine, read <https://twist.langara.ca/record=b1118730~S1>. | For more information, consider Israeli academic Ilan Pappé's *[The Ethnic Cleansing of Palestine](https://twist.langara.ca/record=b1118730~S1).* |
