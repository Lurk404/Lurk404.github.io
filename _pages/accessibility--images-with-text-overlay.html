Instead of adding complicated instructions to the alt text field of a screenshot, consider adding the the text as readable text floating on top of the image. In the example below, sighted users can follow the steps "stamped" on the image. However, as the instructions are text, screen reader and text to speech users can also access the information easily.

1Add a Wordpress block​
2Click on Save Draft
3Check the Pending Review box.
