## What are PDFs?

What does PDF stand for? Some say it stands for [Persistent Displeasing Feature](https://contentious.ltd/why-pdfs-are-rubbish/) or [Please Disappear Forever.](https://contentious.ltd/why-pdfs-are-rubbish/)

PDF stands for Portable Document Format. The PDF file is one of the most common file types. The PDF was developed by Adobe in 1992. [According to Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/devnet/acrobat/pdfs/pdf_reference_1-7.pdf), the “PDF is a file format for representing documents in a manner independent of the application software, hardware, and operating system used to create them and of the output device on which they are to be displayed or printed.”

The software, hardware, operating systems, and devices we use today are unimaginably different from those available in 1992. The concept of 'portable' has changed fundamentally as well. Unfortunately, the PDF has changed little since its creation.

PDFs are:

*“Inherently inaccessible, unpleasant to read, and cumbersome to navigate online.”*

*“Disliked by users, not accessible, disconnected from other browsing behaviours.”*

*“Harder to find, use and maintain. More importantly, unless created with sufficient care PDFs can often be bad for accessibility and rarely comply with open standards.”*

The ubiquitous PDF format is harder to find, use, and maintain. Those are negatives for authors and readers. And yet, we appear addicted to them.
