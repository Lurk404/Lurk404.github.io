I teach colleagues how to make accessible content, run hands-on course design sessions for faculty, and speak at educational technology events in BC. Recordings are linked where they exist.

Conference and sector talks

Most of these were at the Educational Technology Users Group (ETUG), which holds two workshops a year on current topics in educational technology, instructional design, and teaching and learning. Newest first.

Accessibility Why

ETUG Spring 2026

A short demonstration of how common assistive technology can and can't work with inaccessible content: cryptic links, colours that can't be told apart, and hard-to-read text. The point is that poor design doesn't create minor inconveniences for a small group of users; it shuts people out.

  • Accessibility Why (recording)
  • The demonstration examples

Mapping Digital Literacy

2025 Digital Learning Strategy Forum

Myself and two colleagues presented an AI agent that maps learning material to the BC Digital Literacy Framework, and a tool that turns the result into a consistent visual stamp. Participants who had already mapped their courses by hand wished they'd had it sooner, and the discussion turned to customizing the stamp and keeping it accessible. More about the tools: Digital Literacy Framework Mapper and stamp generator.

  • Digital Learning Strategy vodcast, day 1: guest with hosts Tracy Roberts and Britt Dzioba (recording, 32:39)

Digital Accessibility On-demand

ETUG Spring 2025

An introduction to Digital Accessibility On-demand, an open book that sends authors straight to the instructions they need for PowerPoint, Word, and Brightspace content. The BCcampus Pressbooks server was down during the session, so instead of a demonstration I introduced the book's open licence and recruited collaborators to add chapters for Canvas, Moodle, and Blackboard.

  • Digital Accessibility On-demand (recording, 8:31)

The Art of Storytelling: Personas and Anonymized Examples to Teach Accessibility

ETUG Spring 2024

Langara's accessibility workshops use anonymized examples of real accessibility barriers that Langara students have run into. Concrete examples show workshop participants the effect their content has. This session walked through how I build those examples and demonstrated some common barriers.
