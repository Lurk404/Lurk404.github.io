I built a Brightspace course that guides students who use a screen reader, magnifier, or other assistive technology around the platform. I wrote its content, set up how students are enrolled, and added a pop-up that greets them the first time they enter.

In addition to providing examples to practice with, the course (alongside the AT User Guides) I use this course to collect instructions and demonstrations for students to refer to when they need. The video below is an overview of the course.
