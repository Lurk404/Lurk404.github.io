## Course template and component library

I write and maintain the CSS for the Brightspace course template used across all of Langara's courses, along with a shared library of components (accordions, tabs, and columns) that instructors can add to their own content.

Because every course draws on these styles, a fix made once in the template reaches every course that uses it. That makes the template the most efficient place to improve accessibility and consistency across the college.

## Widgets

- **Instructor contact widget.** Gives students a consistent place to find how to reach their instructor. It comes in several layouts, adapts to screen size, and has documentation so instructors can copy and customize it. It's now deployed college-wide.
- **Help for students.** A popover on the course homepage that points students to support.
- **Student tech quick start.** A short guide to the tools students use, opened from the course homepage.
- **Iframe widget.** For embedding content in a course.

## Pilots with departments

I work with departments on pilot projects, including the instructor contact widget, Brightspace's Activity Feed, and the look and feel of H5P activities. For each pilot I design how it will be evaluated and write the guides that go with it.
