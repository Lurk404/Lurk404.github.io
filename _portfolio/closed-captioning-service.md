## What the service does

When a student needs captions for a course, I take it from referral to finished captions:

1. Receive the referral and contact the instructor.
2. Retrieve the course media.
3. Send it to student captioners or to a captioning vendor.
4. Check the quality of the captions.
5. Deliver the caption files into the course.

I'm the college's single point of contact for captioning. Between May 2021 and August 2026 that meant **84 courses across 34 departments**, **117 faculty and staff** I corresponded with, and **578 messages**.

## A student captioning team

I recruited, hired, trained, and supervised **17 student captioners across 13 semesters**.

## Vendors, funding, and workflow

- I manage the college's accounts with 3Play Media and Rev, including prepayment and credit balances.
- I secured **$2,500 in external funding** for captioning ($2,000 from the Langara Foundation and $500 from the Library) and handle the purchasing and reconciliation.
- Separately, I caption the Library's streaming media collection.
- I advise colleagues on captioning workflow in Kaltura and Brightspace, and take part in the Kaltura community forum through BCNET.
