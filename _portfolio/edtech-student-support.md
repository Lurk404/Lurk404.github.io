## The idea

I proposed a visible, drop-in presence for Educational Technology: a table and pop-up held twice a week where students can bring any tech question. Then I designed the service model, including the schedule, advertised hours, where the table sits, and the referral pathways to other student services such as Tech Tutors, the Library, and IT.

## What students bring

From January to July 2026 the service logged **162 interactions**, and I handled **133 of them (82%)** personally.

About half of those questions (**83 of 162**) were outside EdTech's own remit: account activation, multi-factor authentication, campus Wi-Fi, printing, student ID cards, and finding their way around campus. Rather than turn students away, I resolved what I could on the spot and referred the rest to the right service.

## Measuring it

I built the tracking instrument behind those numbers: a Microsoft Form feeding a linked Excel workbook, with pivot tables and reports by type of issue. It gives the college a record of in-person support demand it didn't have before.

The records also show where self-serve help is missing. In the first six months, **61 interactions** concerned something with no guide or other self-serve resource to point to. Those gaps inform which guides I write next, and I share them with the departments whose services students were asking about.
