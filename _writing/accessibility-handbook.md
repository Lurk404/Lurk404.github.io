The handbook introduces the ideas behind digital accessibility, such as colour contrast and alternative text, then gives detailed instructions for using the accessibility features of specific software (making accessible PowerPoint presentations, for example). It now also includes the material from [Digital Accessibility On-demand](/writing/digital-accessibility-on-demand/).

[Read the Accessibility Handbook for Teaching and Learning](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/accessibilityhandbook/)

## Who uses it

The handbook is openly licensed, so other institutions can copy and adapt it. Pressbooks has recorded **12 copies (clones) across at least seven organizations**, including two systems that serve many institutions:

- eCampusOntario (three copies, two of them retitled as AODA compliance resources)
- Minnesota State
- Atlantic OER Collective
- Maricopa Community Colleges
- Harper College
- ACE Accessibility/UMA
- Digital Learning Toolkit

Pressbooks only began recording copies in 2023, three years after the book was published, so the real number is higher.
