<p class="note">This book has been archived. Its material is now part of the <a href="{{ '/writing/accessibility-handbook/' | relative_url }}">Accessibility Handbook for Teaching and Learning</a>.</p>

A just-in-time resource for finding what you need, when you need it, to create accessible content and improve existing material. It points authors to the specific instructions they need for PowerPoint, Word, and Brightspace content. It's openly licensed, and I [presented it at ETUG Spring 2025](/talks/#conference-talks), where I invited people who use other learning management systems (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard) to add chapters.
